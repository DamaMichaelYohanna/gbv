Physical Abuse

Physical

Involves physical force that hurts you or threatens your safety. This includes hitting, punching, slapping, kicking, choking, or using physical restraint. No one has the right to hurt your body.

Sexual Violence

Sexual

Any non-consensual sexual activity or contact. It includes rape, sexual coercion, unwanted touching, or harassment. Consent must be voluntary, enthusiastic, and can be withdrawn at any time.

Emotional & Psychological Abuse

Emotional

Non-physical behaviors such as constant criticism, threats, gaslighting, extreme jealousy, control of who you see or talk to, and verbal insults. It damages self-esteem and limits personal autonomy.

Economic / Financial Abuse

Economic

Controlling your ability to acquire, use, or maintain money and financial resources. This includes withholding allowance, blocking access to bank accounts, preventing you from keeping a job, or stealing your cash.

Harmful Traditional Practices

Cultural

Harmful customary or cultural practices including Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), child/forced marriage, dowry violence, and humiliating widowhood rites. These violate bodily integrity and are prohibited by law.

Tech-Facilitated / Online GBV

Digital

Online harassment, cyberstalking, non-consensual distribution of intimate images, digital surveillance via stalkerware, and impersonation. Digital abuse is recognized and actionable under Nigerian cybercrime laws.

The VAPP Act (Nigeria)

Legal Rights

The Violence Against Persons (Prohibition) Act protects you from all forms of violence, including domestic violence, rape, abandonment, and emotional abuse. You have the right to seek justice, file police reports, and apply for restraining orders.
