RESOURCE HUB & KNOWLEDGE BASE

Recognized Forms of Gender-Based Violence

Familiarize yourself with the definitions, warning signs, and university sanctions associated with different types of abuse under Nigerian law and NSUK regulations.

Physical Abuse

Physical

Using physical force that causes bodily harm, pain, or injury. This includes hitting, slapping, punching, kicking, choking, burning, pushing, or using dangerous weapons. No one has the right to touch your body with violence.

⚖️ Criminal offense under the VAPP Act & grounds for immediate university expulsion or dismissal.

Sexual Harassment & Assault

Zero Tolerance

Any unwanted sexual advance, non-consensual touching, rape, or coercion. Crucially includes "Sex-for-Marks" and academic extortion where grades, admissions, or positions are conditioned upon sexual favors.

⚖️ Strict liability: NSUK Anti-Sexual Harassment Policy provides immediate suspension and prosecution.

Emotional & Psychological Abuse

Psychological

Coercive behavior aimed at destroying self-esteem and independence: constant verbal insults, intimidation, threats of academic or physical ruin, gaslighting, stalking, and forced isolation from friends and family.

⚖️ Recognized form of domestic and interpersonal violence under the VAPP Act.

Economic & Financial Abuse

Economic

Controlling or denying access to money, withholding school fees or maintenance allowances to force compliance, forbidding lawful employment, or stealing scholarships and allowances.

⚖️ Prohibited economic abuse under the VAPP Act (Section 12).

Harmful Traditional Practices

Customary

Harmful customary rites violating bodily integrity and human rights: Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), forced/underage marriage, dowry-related violence, and degrading widowhood practices.

⚖️ Expressly outlawed with criminal penalties under Federal and State VAPP legislation.

Tech-Facilitated / Online GBV

Cyber Law

Cyberstalking, non-consensual sharing or threats of sharing intimate photos/videos ("revenge porn"), online doxxing, harassment via WhatsApp/social media, and installing tracking spyware on phones.

⚖️ Actionable under the Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act of Nigeria.

PERSONAL SAFETY & PRECAUTIONS

Campus Safety Guidance & Planning

Essential safety protocols designed for university students, campus hostel residents, and off-campus community members.

Step 01

Hostel & Room Exit Routes

Identify at least two clear exits from your room or lodge. During arguments or volatile encounters, stay clear of kitchens or small locked bathrooms.

Step 02

Emergency Code Word

Establish a covert code word or distress text phrase with a trusted roommate, coursemate, or family member who will notify campus security immediately.

Step 03

Digital Privacy & Safe Exit

Use incognito browsing when seeking help. Remember you can hit the ESC key or Quick Exit button at any moment to hide this screen instantly.

Step 04

Emergency Go-Bag & Documents

Keep student ID, admission documents, birth certificates, daily medications, and emergency transport cash stored securely with a trusted friend.
