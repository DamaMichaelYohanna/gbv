Physical Abuse

Physical

Inflicting bodily harm or threat of physical force: hitting, punching, slapping, kicking, choking, or weapon violence. You have the right to bodily safety.

Sexual Harassment & Assault

Campus Zero Tolerance

Any non-consensual sexual act, forced contact, unwanted touching, or sexual extortion ("sex for marks"). NSUK regulations strictly prohibit and prosecute these offenses.

Emotional & Psychological Abuse

Emotional

Non-physical behaviors including constant degradation, threats, gaslighting, extreme jealousy, forced social isolation, and academic blackmail.

Economic / Financial Abuse

Economic

Controlling or withholding money, school tuition, or living allowances to maintain coercive control and dependency.

Harmful Cultural Practices

Customary

Customary practices violating human rights: Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), child/forced marriage, and degrading widowhood rites.

Online & Cyber GBV

Digital Safety

Cyberstalking, non-consensual sharing or threats of intimate photos, online harassment, and digital surveillance via stalkerware.

The VAPP Act & Student Rights

Legal Rights

The Violence Against Persons (Prohibition) Act criminalizes all forms of GBV in Nigeria. Survivors have rights to protection orders, medical care, and legal aid.
